# Moonhush Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Moonhush: Sleep Sounds ("the app") plays relaxing sounds to help you fall asleep. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, send to a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Information stored on your device

Your saved sound mixes and sleep timer setting are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

The app asks for permission to show notifications so that playback controls appear on your lock screen and sounds keep playing while the screen is off. It does not use your microphone, camera, location or contacts.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/dohasimi-code/moonhush/issues>.
